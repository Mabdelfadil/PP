Privacy Policy — Security Risk Assessment Tool (SART)

Effective Date: October 16, 2026

Security Risk Assessment Tool (SART) ("the Application") is an industrial security risk assessment application guided by the ANSI/API Standard 780 methodology.

1. Local-Only Data Storage (Zero Data Collection)

Because facility security risk assessments contain sensitive infrastructure information, SART is engineered on a strict Offline-First, Local-Storage Architecture. All projects, assets, pathways, team rosters, threat assessments, vulnerability scores, and generated PDF/CSV reports are stored exclusively locally on your device. We do not collect, transmit, store, or share any of your personal data or facility assessment data on external servers.

2. In-App Purchases & Billing

Upgrades to the SART PRO Enterprise License are processed securely and directly by Google Play Billing. The Application does not access, process, or store your credit card, bank account, or payment instrument details.

3. Permissions Used

The Application only uses local storage permissions to save your projects and export your generated PDF and Excel (.CSV) reports to your device's Downloads folder, and the standard Google Play Billing permission (com.android.vending.BILLING) to verify PRO license upgrades.

4. Third-Party Sharing & Advertising

SART contains no third-party advertisements, no behavioral trackers, and does not sell or share user data with any third party.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy or the Application, please contact us through the developer support email listed on our official Google Play Store page.
